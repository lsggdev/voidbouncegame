Tips for the hardest levels

Ten habits that turn a frustrating level into a clean run.

  1. Watch one full rotation first. Obstacles repeat. Let go and watch the pattern turn once before you commit.
  2. Short holds beat long ones. Roll in small bursts so you can stop the moment something lines up against you.
  3. Move on the bounce, not the gap. Start rolling slightly before the gap reaches you; the ball needs a moment to get there.
  4. Use the sound. The soundtrack follows the tunnel's rhythm. Many patterns are easier to time by ear than by eye.
  5. Look one obstacle ahead. Clearing a gap into a closed wall is the most common way to lose a life.
  6. Learn the modifiers. When a level speeds up, reverses or narrows, the same obstacle needs a different rhythm.
  7. Take a break when lives run low. Lives come back every 20 minutes, and a rested reaction time is worth more than a rushed retry.
  8. Do the daily challenge. It pays coins every day and is the quickest way to unlock skins without spending anything.
  9. Replay old levels for stars. Easy stars add up and keep you sharp for the bonus stages.
  10. Play fullscreen on desktop. A bigger tunnel makes gaps easier to read. Use the fullscreen button under the game.
