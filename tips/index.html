Tips for the hardest levels

Twelve habits that turn a frustrating level into a clean run.

  1. Watch one full rotation first. Obstacles repeat. Let go and watch the pattern turn once before you commit.
  2. Roll in short bursts. Small holds let you stop the moment something lines up against you.
  3. Start a little early. The ball needs a moment to reach the gap, so begin rolling just before it arrives.
  4. Look one obstacle ahead. Clearing a gap straight into a closed wall is the most common way to lose a life, especially in walls in a row.
  5. Stop on solid floor. From level 95 the floor has holes. When you wait, make sure the ball is over solid ground.
  6. Use the sound. The soundtrack follows the tunnel's rhythm. In dark stretches you can time gaps by ear.
  7. Re-learn mirrored sections. A mirrored cylinder flips patterns you already know, so watch before you move.
  8. Expect the flip. In an upside-down cylinder the ball bounces on the ceiling; the gap you need is on the other side.
  9. Do not wait too long in a collapse. From level 300 the tunnel can collapse behind you, so waiting forever is not an option.
  10. Take a break when lives run low. A life returns every 20 minutes, and a rested reaction time beats a rushed retry.
  11. Play the daily challenge. It pays coins every day and is the fastest free way to unlock skins.
  12. Go fullscreen on desktop. A bigger tunnel makes gaps easier to read. Use the button under the game.
