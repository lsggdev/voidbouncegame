Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-04.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit voidbouncegame.com and play Void Bounce.

Game saves

Your progress (levels, coins, skins and settings) is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Advertising

The site shows ads from Adsterra and may show ads from Google AdSense. These partners and their vendors may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure performance, and may collect your IP address, browser and device information. Learn more in Adsterra's privacy policy and how Google uses data in ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Stripe on its own checkout page. We never see your card details. Stripe shares with us what is needed to deliver your item and handle support, such as the product, the amount and your email. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data held about you. For anything related to a purchase, reply to your Stripe receipt email. For other requests, see the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
