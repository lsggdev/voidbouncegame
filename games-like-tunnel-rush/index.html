Games like Tunnel Rush

Neon tunnels, fast reflexes and one-more-try runs, without a download.

Tunnel Rush made the neon tunnel a classic of browser gaming: you fly down a spinning tube and dodge whatever comes at you. If you enjoy that feeling of speed and colour, these games scratch the same itch in different ways.

1. Void Bounce

Void Bounce keeps the neon tunnel but changes who is in control. The ball only rolls while you hold, so instead of dodging at full speed you read the tunnel and time your move. It has 850 levels with an ending each, plus an endless mode for pure Tunnel Rush-style runs. It is free and plays here in the browser.

2. Slope

A ball races down a neon slope and you steer left and right to stay on the track. Like Tunnel Rush, it never slows down; unlike it, falling off the edge is the main danger.

3. Run 3

An endless runner set in a space tunnel where you can run on the walls and ceiling. It adds platforming and a long campaign to the tunnel idea.

4. Geometry Dash

A one-button rhythm platformer. The neon look and the brutal timing will feel familiar, though the action is side-on instead of inside a tunnel.

5. Helix Jump

A ball bounces down a spinning tower and you rotate the tower to drop it through gaps. It shares the spinning, one-input simplicity. See our list of games like Helix Jump.

6. Color-matching tunnel games

Many browser games ask you to pass only through the gate of your colour as the tunnel speeds up. They mix Tunnel Rush's speed with a colour-reading twist.
