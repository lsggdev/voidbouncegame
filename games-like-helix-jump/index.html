Games like Helix Jump

Bouncing balls, one-tap controls and short levels you can finish on a coffee break.

Helix Jump turned a simple idea into a hit: a ball bounces down a spinning tower and you turn the tower to drop it through the gaps. These games keep that bouncing, one-input feel.

1. Void Bounce

Turn Helix Jump on its side and put it inside a neon tunnel. In Void Bounce the ball bounces on its own and you hold to roll it forward through the gaps. It has 850 short levels, 15 obstacle types and 50 ball skins, and it is free in your browser.

2. Stack Ball

You smash a ball down through a spinning stack of plates, holding to break through and releasing before you hit the dark sections. It is the closest in feel to Helix Jump.

3. Color Switch

Tap to bounce a ball upward through rotating shapes, passing only through the matching colour. Same one-tap rhythm, different direction.

4. Ball games with a tower or tube

Many browser games put a ball on a rotating tower or tube and ask you to avoid red or spiked sections. They are quick to learn and good for short sessions.

5. Geometry Dash

A one-button platformer with demanding timing. It is harder and longer than Helix Jump, but the satisfaction of a clean run is similar.

What Void Bounce adds

  • Control over time: you choose when the ball moves, so you can stop and study the next gap.
  • A finish line: every level ends, with up to three stars to collect.
  • Variety: 13 modifiers, from mirrored to upside-down sections, keep late levels fresh. See the levels guide.
