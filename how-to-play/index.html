How to play Void Bounce

Everything you need to know before your first run, in about two minutes of reading.

The controls

Void Bounce uses one input. The ball bounces on its own inside a spinning neon tunnel. Hold the mouse button, your finger or the space bar to roll forward. Let go and the ball stops, still bouncing, while the obstacles keep turning around it.

Every level is a short tunnel. Reach the end without touching an obstacle to clear it. Touch one and the run ends.

Lives

You start with three lives and lose one each time a run ends. A life comes back every 20 minutes. When you run out you can wait, refill a life with coins you earned, or get more in the shop.

Coins and the shop

You earn coins by clearing levels, finishing quests and playing the daily challenge. Spend them on new ball skins in the skin machine, on 3D accessories, or on a life when you need one. Coin packs are also for sale if you want to unlock cosmetics faster.

Game modes

  • Levels: 500 main stages and 350 bonus stages, each with stars to collect.
  • Endless: one tunnel that never ends. Your best distance goes to the leaderboard.
  • Daily challenge: a new run every day with three attempts.

Saving

Progress is saved in your browser. Use the same browser and device to keep your levels, coins and skins.
