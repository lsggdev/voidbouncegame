How to play Void Bounce

Controls, lives, coins and game modes, explained in two minutes.

Controls: one button

Void Bounce uses a single input on every device. The ball bounces on its own inside a spinning neon tunnel.

  • Computer: hold the left mouse button or the space bar to roll; release to stop.
  • Phone or tablet: touch and hold anywhere on the game; lift your finger to stop.

When you stop, the ball keeps bouncing in place while the obstacles keep turning. That is the whole skill: wait for the gap, then roll.

Clearing a level

Each level is a short tunnel. Reach the end without touching an obstacle and you clear it, earning up to three stars. Touch one and the run ends. Restarts are instant.

Lives

You start with three lives and lose one when a run ends. A life comes back every 20 minutes. If you run out, you can wait, spend coins to refill a life, or get more in the shop.

Coins, skins and accessories

Coins come from clearing levels, quests and the daily challenge. Spend them in the skin machine to unlock new balls, or on 3D accessories like a top hat or a gamer headset. Coin packs are on sale too if you want cosmetics faster.

Game modes

  • Levels: 500 main levels in 10 worlds, then 350 bonus levels. See the levels and obstacles guide.
  • Endless: one tunnel with no end. Your best distance goes to the leaderboard.
  • Daily challenge: a new run every day with three attempts.

Saving your progress

Progress is stored in your browser. Play on the same browser and device to keep your levels, coins and skins.
