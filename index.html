A tunnel game you play with one button

Void Bounce is a free online arcade game set inside a spinning neon tunnel. Your ball bounces on its own. You decide only one thing: when it rolls forward. Hold to move, let go to wait, and slip through each gap before the walls, blades and spikes turn back into your path.

It plays instantly in any modern browser, on a phone, tablet, Chromebook or computer, with no download and no sign-up. Runs are short, restarts are instant, and every level has a finish line, so there is always one more try in reach.

How to play

  1. HoldPress and hold the mouse, your finger or the space bar. The ball rolls forward through the tunnel.
  2. Let goRelease to stop. The ball keeps bouncing in place while the obstacles turn around it.
  3. Slip throughRoll again when the gap lines up. Reach the end of the tunnel to clear the level and collect stars.

The main campaign moves through ten worlds, each with its own colors and sky: Laboratory, Greenhouse, Forge, Glacier, Neon, Desert, Jungle, Abyss, Core and Aurora. The 350 bonus levels cycle through them again with harder layouts. The levels guide lists when each obstacle and modifier first appears.

If you like Tunnel Rush or Helix Jump

Void Bounce sits between two familiar ideas: the neon tunnel of reflex games like Tunnel Rush, and the single-tap bounce of Helix Jump. The difference is control. Nothing moves until you hold, so the challenge is reading the tunnel and timing your roll, not reacting to a ball that never stops. Frequently asked questions

Is Void Bounce free to play?

Yes. All 850 levels are free to play in your browser. Coin packs, extra lives and a legendary skin pack are optional and sold through Stripe; you never need them to finish the game.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Void Bounce runs directly in the browser on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers. There is no install and no sign-up.

How do I play Void Bounce?

The ball bounces on its own. Hold the mouse button, your finger or the space bar to roll forward, and let go to stop and wait for the gap in the tunnel to open.

How many levels are there?

850: 500 main levels across 10 worlds, plus 350 harder bonus levels. There is also an endless mode and a daily challenge.

How do lives work?

You have three lives. One comes back every 20 minutes. You can also refill a life with coins you earn in the game, or get more in the shop.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in Safari, Chrome and other modern mobile browsers. Use the fullscreen button for a bigger tunnel.

Is my progress saved?

Levels, coins and skins are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data or using private mode resets them.

Is Void Bounce like Tunnel Rush or Helix Jump?

It shares the neon tunnel of Tunnel Rush and the one-button bounce of Helix Jump, but you control when the ball moves, and every level has an ending. See our page on games like Tunnel Rush.

Is there a version without ads?

Yes. A paid download for Windows, macOS and Linux on itch.io has no ads and no purchases, and works offline.
