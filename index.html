Void Bounce

A neon tunnel, a bouncing ball and one button. Time your roll through 850 levels, right here in your browser.

Hold to roll, release to wait.

How to play

  1. HoldPress and hold the mouse, your finger or the space bar. The ball rolls forward through the tunnel.
  2. Let goRelease to stop. The ball keeps bouncing in place while the obstacles turn around it.
  3. Slip throughRoll again when the gap opens. Reach the end of the tunnel to clear the level.

Questions

Is Void Bounce free to play?

Yes. Every one of the 850 levels can be played for free right here in your browser. Optional coin packs, extra lives and a legendary skin pack are sold through Stripe, but you never need them to finish the game.

How do I control the ball?

The ball bounces on its own. Hold the mouse button, your finger or the space bar to roll forward, and let go to stop and wait for the gap in the tunnel to open.

How do lives work?

You have three lives. A life comes back every 20 minutes, and you can also refill with coins earned in the game or buy more in the shop.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Void Bounce runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, iPad, Chromebook, Windows, Mac and Linux. No download or sign-up is needed.

Is my progress saved?

Your levels, coins and skins are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data or using private mode will reset them.

Is there a download version?

Yes. A paid download for Windows, macOS and Linux is available on itch.io. It works offline and has no ads and no purchases.
